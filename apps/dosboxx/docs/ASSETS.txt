DOSBox-X is fetched into:
  apps/dosboxx/build/dosbox-x-src/

The built main binary is:
  apps/dosboxx/build/dosbox-x

Windows media is not bundled. Add your own licensed images under:
  apps/win31/images/system.img
  apps/win95/images/system.img

Exit controls on Gemini:
  Ctrl+F9 exits DOSBox-X
  Ctrl+F10 toggles mouse capture

Touch-to-mouse is enabled as a first-pass absolute pointer mapping.
