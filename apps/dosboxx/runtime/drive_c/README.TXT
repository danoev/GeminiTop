DOSBox-X for Gemini SP7021
==========================

This launcher entry starts a plain DOSBox-X shell without proprietary media.

Mounted paths:
  C:\  runtime/drive_c
  D:\  runtime/shared

Controls:
  Ctrl+F9   Exit DOSBox-X back to Gemini Launcher
  Ctrl+F10  Toggle mouse capture

Windows images are launched from the separate "Windows 3.1" and
"Windows 95" launcher entries after you add your own licensed images.
